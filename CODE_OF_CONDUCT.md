# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation respectful and inclusive.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful
- No harassment
- No offensive behavior

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Maintainers are responsible for enforcing the rules.

## Scope
This applies within all project spaces.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in warnings or removal.

## Enforcement Guidelines
- Warning
- Temporary ban
- Permanent ban

## Attribution
This code of conduct is adapted from common open-source standards.
